GPD

Technology designed for reliable, practical geospatial workflows.

Geospatial Portal Data

Map and analyze location data with clarity

GPD is a geospatial platform that uses authorized location data for mapping, infrastructure planning, logistics, and area-level analysis.

It integrates multiple data sources — including weather and environmental data — through layered mapping, giving teams a clearer view for planning and reporting.

Key Capabilities

What GPD delivers

Practical features for mapping, planning, and area-level analysis.

Layered mapping

Combine multiple data layers into a single, clear geospatial view.

Location analysis

Analyze authorized location data for coverage, logistics, and planning.

Source integration

Integrate weather, environmental, and other authorized data sources.

Use Cases

Where GPD fits

  • Infrastructure and service-coverage planning
  • Logistics and route planning
  • Area-level analysis and reporting
  • Environmental and weather-informed planning
