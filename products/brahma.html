BRAHMA

Technology designed for reliable, practical communication workflows.

Broadcast WhatsApp Massive

Large-scale messaging, organized and reliable

BRAHMA is a multi-channel messaging platform designed to help organizations deliver large-scale, coordinated communication through modern messaging applications such as WhatsApp and Telegram.

It supports structured message distribution and effective audience engagement, giving teams a dependable way to reach the right people across digital messaging networks.

Key Capabilities

What BRAHMA delivers

Practical features built for large-scale, organized messaging across multiple channels.

Multi-channel delivery

Distribute messages across WhatsApp, Telegram, and other modern channels from one platform.

Large-scale broadcast

Send organized, high-volume communication to large audiences efficiently.

Engagement insights

Track delivery and engagement to understand how communication performs.

Use Cases

Where BRAHMA fits

  • Customer notifications and service announcements
  • Organizational outreach and community engagement
  • Event, campaign, and program communication
  • Internal updates across large teams
