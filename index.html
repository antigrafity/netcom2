Who We Are

Engineers for the Layer Everything Else Depends On

Applications, data, and users all depend on the infrastructure underneath them. When servers slow down or the network drops, the whole organization feels it. Netcom Scinergy Newsantara is an infrastructure and network engineering company. We survey your current environment, design the right capacity, install and configure the equipment, and keep it running with structured maintenance. Our engineers work hands-on, from the server rack to the branch office router.

Why Netcom Scinergy

Engineering Standards You Can Check

Infrastructure work is only as good as its details. These are the standards every Netcom project follows.

Site Survey Before Quotation

We inspect the physical site, existing equipment, and traffic before proposing anything, so the design fits reality.

Capacity Planning

Compute, storage, and bandwidth are sized for today's load plus planned growth, avoiding both bottlenecks and overspending.

Redundancy by Design

Critical links, power, and servers are designed without single points of failure wherever the budget allows.

Tested Before Handover

Every installation goes through acceptance testing: failover, throughput, and configuration checks, signed off with you.

Complete Documentation

Network diagrams, rack layouts, IP plans, and configuration backups are delivered with every project.

Maintenance That Prevents Downtime

Scheduled preventive maintenance and health reports catch problems before users notice them.

How We Deliver

Survey. Blueprint. Build. Run.

A four-phase engineering method with a clear deliverable at the end of each phase, so you always know what has been done and what comes next.

Phase 1

Survey

Site visit, inventory of existing equipment, and performance baseline. Deliverable: assessment report.

Phase 2

Blueprint

Architecture, bill of materials, capacity plan, and cut-over schedule. Deliverable: design document.

Phase 3

Build

Installation, configuration, migration, and acceptance testing. Deliverable: as-built documentation.

Phase 4

Run

Preventive maintenance, monitoring, and support. Deliverable: periodic health reports.
