Survey. Blueprint. Build. Run.

Four phases, the same on every project. Each one ends with a document you can review, approve, and keep.

Why We Use the Same Method

No Surprises Between Proposal and Go-Live

Infrastructure projects usually go wrong for one of two reasons: someone guessed instead of measuring, or nobody wrote down what was installed. Our method avoids both.

Phase 1

Survey

On-site inspection of rooms, racks, cabling, and equipment; inventory of servers and network devices; measurement of utilization, bandwidth, and Wi-Fi coverage. Deliverable: assessment report with findings and risks.

Phase 1

Requirements

Interviews with IT and business users to confirm growth plans, critical applications, acceptable downtime, and budget range.

Phase 2

Blueprint

Target architecture, network diagrams, capacity calculations, and bill of materials. Deliverable: design document and quotation.

Phase 2

Cut-Over Plan

Step-by-step schedule for installation and migration, with maintenance windows and rollback procedures agreed in advance.

Phase 3

Build

Equipment installation, cabling, configuration, and workload migration by Netcom engineers, following the approved design.

Phase 3

Acceptance Test

Failover, throughput, coverage, and configuration tests performed with your team. Deliverable: signed test report and as-built documentation.

Phase 4

Run

Preventive maintenance visits, monitoring, and support under a maintenance agreement. Deliverable: periodic health reports.

Our Rule

Measure before designing. Test before handing over. Document everything.
