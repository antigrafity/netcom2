An Engineering Company for Business Technology

Netcom Scinergy Newsantara supplies, installs, and maintains the hardware, software, and systems that Indonesian organizations rely on every day, from a single license to a full server room.

Company Profile

Engineers First, Suppliers Second

Netcom Scinergy was started by engineers, and engineers still run it. The same people who recommend a product are the ones who install it, configure it, and come back when it needs attention. That is why we only sell what we know how to support.

We serve organizations of every size, from a single office that needs one reliable system to multi-branch companies with dozens of sites, applications, and integrations. Our offices are in Jakarta and Bekasi.

  • IoT sensors, CCTV, and access control
  • Hosting, monitoring, and maintenance
How We Work

Numbers Before and After

We don't guess. Before we start, we measure how things work today: how the process runs, how fast the systems respond, and how much capacity is left. When the work is done, we measure again and show you the difference.

Every project ends with full documentation (source code, diagrams, IP plans, rack layouts, and configuration backups), so your team, or any future engineer, knows exactly what is installed and how it is configured.
