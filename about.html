An Engineering Company for IT Infrastructure

Netcom Scinergy Newsantara designs, builds, and maintains the servers, storage, cloud environments, and networks that Indonesian organizations rely on every day.

Company Profile

Hands-On Engineers, Not Just Consultants

Netcom Scinergy was started by network and infrastructure engineers, and engineers still do the work: surveying sites, sizing capacity, racking and configuring equipment, migrating workloads, and maintaining everything afterward.

We serve organizations of every size, from a single head office that needs a reliable server room to multi-branch companies connecting dozens of sites to the cloud. Our offices are in Jakarta and Bekasi.

  • Load balancing and application hosting
How We Work

Measured Before, Tested After

We don't guess. Before we start, we measure how your environment is doing today: utilization, bandwidth, error rates, and spare capacity. When the work is done, we measure again and show you the difference.

Every project ends with as-built documentation (diagrams, IP plans, rack layouts, and configuration backups), so your team, or any future engineer, knows exactly what is installed and how it is configured.
