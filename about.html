Who We Are

A technology company focused on advanced integrated systems

PT Netcom Scinergy Newsantara is a technology company focused on the development and implementation of advanced Information Technology solutions, particularly in integrated systems and specialized technology equipment. Our expertise lies in delivering end-to-end solutions that combine both hardware and software to support complex operational environments.

Recognizing that technology solutions developed abroad often require significant adaptation to align with local conditions, since 1995 we have engaged in continuous research and development with various partners to design tailored technology systems and integrated equipment better suited to real-world conditions in Indonesia.
