Connect What You Have. Move What You Must.

Integrating applications that don't talk to each other, and moving systems, data, and servers from one place to another without stopping the business.

Overview

Planned Down to the Maintenance Window

Most companies run a mix of systems from different vendors and different years. Data gets copied between them by hand, and moving any of them feels risky. We map how your systems connect today, design the integrations or the move, and write a cut-over plan with rollback steps agreed in advance.

Every migration is rehearsed before it happens for real, and every integration is monitored after go-live, so problems surface on our side, not your users'.

Common Problems

Sound Familiar?

  • Staff retype the same data into several systems
  • Applications from different vendors cannot share data
  • A server or data center has to move with minimal downtime
  • Data quality in the old system is unknown
  • Two companies now need to work on one IT environment
  • A past migration failed and nobody wants to try again
Results

What You Get

  • Less manual data entry and fewer copy errors
  • A rehearsed cut-over plan with rollback steps
  • Reconciliation reports proving the data arrived intact
  • Downtime limited to agreed maintenance windows
  • One sign-in across connected systems
  • Documentation of every interface and data flow
