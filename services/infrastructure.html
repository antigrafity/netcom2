Servers, Storage, and Cloud Built for High Availability

The compute and storage foundation your applications run on, sized from real measurements and designed without single points of failure.

Overview

From Server Room to Hybrid Cloud

Aging servers, full storage, and untested backups are among the most common causes of business downtime. We assess your current workloads, plan capacity for the next three to five years, and build an environment that is easier to manage.

Whether you keep everything on-premise, move to the cloud, or run a mix of both, we design the architecture, supply and install the hardware, migrate the workloads, and hand over complete documentation.

Common Problems

Sound Familiar?

  • Servers are out of warranty or running at full capacity
  • Storage keeps running out and performance is degrading
  • Backups exist but recovery has never been tested
  • Too many physical servers that are hard to manage
  • Unclear which workloads should move to the cloud

Results
