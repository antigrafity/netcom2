Overview

Connectivity as Part of the Complete Solution

As organizations become more distributed and application-dependent, connectivity must support performance, availability, scalability, and the way the business actually operates.

Netcom Scinergy helps customers plan, integrate, and optimize connectivity environments as part of the wider enterprise architecture. We consider how network and communication requirements relate to applications, infrastructure, locations, users, and operational priorities so connectivity supports the complete solution rather than existing as a separate technology layer.

Business Challenges Addressed

Challenges We Help Solve

  • Inconsistent connectivity performance across locations or user groups
  • New applications or services increasing network demand
  • Expansion to additional sites, offices, facilities, or operating locations
  • Disconnected communication environments that are difficult to manage consistently
  • Connectivity changes that must be coordinated with infrastructure or application initiatives
  • Need for a more scalable network foundation as the organization grows
