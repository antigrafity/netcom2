The Software Layer That Keeps Infrastructure Running

Operating systems, hypervisors, databases, and management tools installed, configured, patched, and kept up to date.

Overview

Properly Configured From Day One

Many performance and security problems come from platform software that was installed quickly and never tuned. We install and configure operating systems, virtualization platforms, and databases according to vendor best practice and your security policies.

We also handle upgrades and patching, so your platforms stay supported and secure without disrupting daily operations.

Common Problems

Sound Familiar?

  • Operating systems or hypervisors are out of vendor support
  • Platforms were installed with default settings and never tuned
  • Patches are applied irregularly or not at all
  • User accounts and permissions are inconsistent
  • No central view of servers and their health
  • Unclear licensing position
