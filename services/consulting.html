RF & Communication Consulting

Understanding your operational needs and RF environment before a single component is specified — the advisory foundation of every successful communication system.

Overview

Get the Foundation Right From the Start

Consulting is where we assess what your operation actually needs, survey the sites and RF conditions, and plan a communication approach that fits. This upfront work reduces risk, avoids over- or under-engineering, and sets clear targets for the design phase that follows.

Services in this category

What Consulting Includes

Eight advisory services covering assessment, survey, planning, and architecture.

01

Communication Needs Assessment

Defining operational, coverage, and capacity requirements before design begins.

02

RF Site Survey

On-site survey of sites, structures, and existing RF conditions.

03

RF Environment Assessment

Assessing interference, spectrum use, and propagation conditions.

04

Coverage Assessment

Evaluating current coverage, gaps, and target coverage areas.

05

Frequency, coverage, and capacity planning aligned to requirements.

System-level planning across the whole operation.
