Keeping It Running After Go-Live

Hosting, monitoring, security, and maintenance for the systems we build, and for the ones we didn't.

Overview

The Run Phase Is Where Projects Prove Themselves

A system that worked on handover day still needs patches, monitoring, capacity checks, and someone to call when it misbehaves. We host and look after applications and infrastructure under clear service agreements, with response times written down.

Every maintenance visit and incident is logged, and you receive regular health reports showing what we checked, what we changed, and what we recommend next.
