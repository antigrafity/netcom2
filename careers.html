Build Enterprise Technology That Has to Work

We are a systems integrator. Our work involves connecting infrastructure, software platforms, systems, connectivity, and applications into environments organizations depend on every day.

Working at Netcom Scinergy

Technical Depth, Across More Than One Layer

Because we work as an integrator rather than a single-product vendor, our teams see the whole environment — infrastructure, platforms, networks, applications, and the dependencies between them. That breadth is unusual, and it is the fastest way we know to build engineers and managers who understand how enterprise technology actually behaves in production.

We work directly with customer teams. Requirements get clarified in the room, trade-offs get explained, and the people who design a solution are usually the people who see it through implementation. We would rather give you ownership and context than hand you a narrow task.

The work is practical. Environments are rarely greenfield, deadlines are real, and decisions have to hold up after handover. We look for people who are comfortable with that and who care about the result being operable, not just delivered.

What We Look For

The Qualities That Matter Here

Technical skill is the starting point. What separates people in integration work is how they handle ambiguity, dependencies, and other teams.

Ownership

You follow a problem through to a working outcome rather than stopping at the boundary of your role.

Clarity

You can explain a technical decision to an engineer and to a business stakeholder, and adjust the framing for each.

Judgement

You weigh constraints honestly, flag risk early, and choose the option that holds up in operation.

Collaboration

Integration work crosses teams and vendors. You coordinate without losing the thread.

Project Manager

Requirements

  • Bachelor's degree or higher in Information Technology, Computer Science, Engineering, or a related field.
  • Minimum 5 years of experience in project management, with a strong portfolio of end-to-end delivery across multi-stakeholder IT projects.
  • Experience managing projects for public sector institutions or regulated enterprise clients is an advantage.
  • Experience managing cybersecurity, system integration, or large-scale network and system monitoring projects is an advantage.
  • Proven success managing cross-functional teams and delivering projects in regulated, high-security, or mission-critical settings.
  • Certified in PMP®, PRINCE2®, or other internationally recognized project management frameworks are highly valued.
  • Solid understanding of both Agile and Waterfall methodologies; capable of tailoring hybrid approaches depending on project needs.
  • Excellent communication, leadership, and conflict resolution skills.
  • Demonstrated ability to manage multiple complex projects simultaneously.
  • Fluent in English and Bahasa Indonesia, both written and verbal.
  • High integrity, strategic mindset, and proactive attitude.
  • Willing to travel domestically if required by project.

Responsibilities

  • Develop and execute comprehensive project plans, risk mitigation strategies, and stakeholder communication frameworks.
  • Drive projects to completion with strong attention to quality, budget control, and resource allocation.
  • Ensure alignment of project deliverables with business objectives, regulatory requirements, and client expectations.
  • Actively monitor project health, identify deviations early, and implement corrective actions swiftly.
  • Manage project scope and change requests with proper documentation and impact analysis.
  • Lead cross-functional project teams, ensuring clarity in roles, timelines, and accountability.
  • Provide regular updates to internal and external stakeholders, including executive summaries and progress reports.
  • Propose and implement innovative improvements to systems, services, or workflows based on post-project evaluation and feedback.
  • Ensure full compliance with project governance, audit standards, and documentation protocols.
