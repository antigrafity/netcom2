Work on Technology People Rely On

We are a systems integrator. Our work covers software, data centers, cloud, networks, and connected devices, built and maintained for organizations that depend on them every day.

Life at Netcom Scinergy

You'll Touch Every Layer, Not Just One

We don't sell one product. We build and connect whole setups: software, servers, networks, and the devices on them. Working here, you'll see how all of it depends on everything else, which is one of the fastest ways to really learn how technology behaves in the real world.

You'll sit across the table from clients, agree requirements with them directly, and explain the trade-offs yourself. Whoever designs a solution usually stays with it until handover. We'd rather trust you with real ownership than hand you a narrow task.

This is hands-on work. Sites rarely start from scratch, dates matter, and whatever you decide has to keep working after we leave. If that sounds good rather than stressful, we should talk.

Who Fits In

What Makes Someone Good Here

Technical ability gets you in the door. What sets people apart is how they handle vague requirements, moving parts, and working with other teams.

Ownership

You chase a problem until it's actually solved, even when the fix sits outside your job title.

Clarity

You can explain the same decision to an engineer and to a finance manager, and both of them get it.

Judgement

You're honest about limits, raise risks early, and pick the option that will still work a year from now.

Collaboration

Most jobs involve several teams and suppliers. You help keep them pointed the same way.

Manager

Project Manager

Requirements

  • At least a bachelor's degree in Information Technology, Computer Science, Engineering, or a similar field.
  • 5+ years managing projects, including IT projects you ran from start to finish with many stakeholders involved.
  • Projects for public institutions or regulated companies are a plus.
  • Background in security, system integration, or large monitoring projects is a plus.
  • A track record of leading mixed teams and delivering in environments where downtime or compliance really matters.
  • PMP®, PRINCE2®, or a similar certification counts strongly in your favor.
  • Comfortable with Agile and Waterfall, and with mixing the two when a project calls for it.
  • Strong at communicating, leading, and settling disagreements.
  • Able to keep several complex projects on track at once.
  • Fluent in English and Bahasa Indonesia, spoken and written.
  • Honest, forward-thinking, and quick to act.
  • Happy to travel within Indonesia when a project needs it.

Responsibilities

  • Write and run project plans, risk plans, and a clear rhythm of updates for everyone involved.
  • Get projects over the line with a close eye on quality, budget, and people's time.
  • Make sure what we deliver matches the client's goals, the rules that apply, and what was promised.
  • Watch progress closely, spot drift early, and correct course fast.
  • Handle scope and change requests properly, with written impact on time and cost.
  • Lead mixed teams so everyone knows their role, their dates, and who owns what.
  • Report progress regularly to management and clients, from one-page summaries to detailed reports.
  • Use lessons from each project to suggest and carry out better ways of working.
  • Keep every project in line with our governance, audit, and documentation rules.

Supervisor

Electronic Embedded System Supervisor

Requirements

  • At least S1 in Electrical Engineering, Computer Engineering, Mechatronics, or a similar field.
  • 2–3 years or more as a Lead Engineer, Supervisor, or Section Head.
  • Can lead a team of engineers.
  • Solves problems and makes decisions with confidence.
  • Has managed projects and project risk.
  • Works well across teams and communicates clearly.
  • Understands embedded system architecture.
  • Skilled in designing and analyzing electronic circuits.
  • Programs microcontrollers and SoCs confidently.
  • Has written low-level firmware, including on an RTOS.

Responsibilities

  • Lead the electronics and embedded engineering team.
  • Shape the embedded architecture across hardware, firmware, and interfaces.
  • Review schematics, PCB layouts, and component choices.
  • Guide firmware work on MCU, SoC, RTOS, and bare-metal targets.
  • Make sure hardware, firmware, and software fit together cleanly.
  • Run prototyping, debugging, and validation.
  • Keep designs in line with technical, safety, and regulatory standards.
  • Write and review design documents, test reports, and bills of materials.
  • Work with QA on testing and on raising product quality.
  • Spot technical risks early and plan how to handle them.
